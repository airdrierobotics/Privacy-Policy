Privacy Policy

Calgary's Own Classifieds — a fair, no-paywall marketplace for Alberta

Effective Date: May 8, 2026  ·  App: ABCTrade  ·  Bundle ID: com.abctrade.app
📋
Section 1
Information We Collect

When you use ABCTrade, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information — your email address and password, authenticated via Firebase Authentication
  • Listing content — photos, descriptions, prices, and other details you submit when creating listings or auctions
  • Messages — content of messages exchanged between users through in-app messaging
  • Verification requests — any identity or verification documents you voluntarily submit
  • Usage data — general app activity such as listings viewed or bids placed
🎯
Section 2
How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Enable you to post listings and participate in auctions
  • Facilitate communication between buyers and sellers
  • Review and process identity verification requests
  • Improve the app and resolve support issues
🔒
Section 3
Data Storage

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Cloud Storage), hosted on Google's infrastructure. By using ABCTrade, you acknowledge Google's data handling as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

🤝
Section 4
Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your data with third parties except:

  • As required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of users or the public
  • With service providers (e.g., Firebase/Google) necessary to operate the app
📣
Section 5
User-Generated Content

Listings, photos, and auction posts you create are visible to other users of the app. Please do not include sensitive personal information in public listings.

🗂️
Section 6
Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may contact us to request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time.

👶
Section 7
Children's Privacy

ABCTrade is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

⚖️
Section 8
Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account
📝
Section 9
Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the effective date at the top of this page when changes are made. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
